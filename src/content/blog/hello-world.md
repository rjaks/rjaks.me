Welcome to my refreshed personal website.

Over the past few years, personal websites have undergone a noticeable cycle. We started with raw HTML/CSS pages, transitioned into heavily animated corporate-style portfolios with complex 3D meshes, and are now swinging back toward something quieter, faster, and more intentional.

## Why the Redesign?

I wanted a digital garden that feels both **technically rooted** and **delightfully simple to read**. 

When I stumbled across minimal developer setups—like Kavin's clean editorial layout—I appreciated how direct everything was. No massive hero banners shouting at you, no five-megabyte JavaScript bundles blocking your render tree, just clear typography, active writing, and real artifacts of what someone is building.
